Here's the detailed breakdown of what's built for the dashboard and Pal Hub pages:

---

## `/dashboard` — Main Dashboard

The dashboard (`src/pages/Dashboard.tsx`) is wrapped in a `DashboardLayout` (3-column: left sidebar, center content, right stats panel). It renders these sections top to bottom:

### 1. Industry-Personalized System Banner
A card that pulls the user's `industry` from the `profiles` table and says "Building Your Complete [Industry] Video System" with an overall completion percentage from the `get_total_system_completion` RPC function.

### 2. SystemCompletionCard
Shows a master progress bar plus 4 individual Pal progress bars (Reel 📱, Evergreen 🌲, Spotlight 🎬, System ⚙️). Each percentage is calculated by querying `user_video_checklist` rows for that Pal and computing `completed / total`. When 100% is reached, a congratulations banner appears.

### 3. HeroBanner
A purple promotional card with the tagline "Automate Your Content Creation with AI-Powered Tools" and a CTA button "Start Creating" that navigates to `/tools/video-series-builder`.

### 4. ToolProgressCards
A 4-column grid showing usage stats for: Series Builder, Persona Generator, Production Assistant, Content Maximizer. Each card shows the usage count and "last used" timestamp, pulled from `credit_transactions` where `transaction_type = 'usage'`.

### 5. ContinueCreating
A list of 5 tool cards (Series Builder, Persona Generator, Production Assistant, Content Maximizer, Engagement Responder) with last-used timestamps and click-to-navigate to `/dashboard/tools/:id`.

### 6. Right Sidebar (DashboardStats)
- **User Profile Card**: Avatar (uses favorite Pal headshot), greeting by time of day, achievement tier icon, streak counter
- **7-Day Activity Chart**: `ActivityChart` component
- **Quick Stats**: Credits used, tools mastered (5+ uses), achievement tier name
- **Top 3 Tools**: Ranked by usage count
- **Favorite Pal Card**: Shows current favorite with option to change

### 7. PalAvatarSelector (Dialog)
A modal to switch your favorite Pal, stored in `profiles.favorite_pal`.

## `/dashboard/pals/:palId` — Pal Hub

The Pal Hub (`src/pages/dashboard/PalHub.tsx`) is the per-Pal detail page with video checklists. Here's exactly what each Pal contains:

### Checklist Items by Pal

**Reel Pal (12 items, 2 categories):**
- Social Reels (6): About the Business, About the Service/Product, How This Helps, Client Testimonials, Behind the Scenes, Educational Content
- Web Video (6): About Video, Pricing Explainer, Process Explainer, FAQ Video, Service Overview, Portfolio Showcase

**Evergreen Pal (10 items, 2 categories):**
- Educational Content (5): How-To Tutorial, Industry Insights, Best Practices, Common Mistakes, Getting Started Guide
- Long-Form Value (5): Ultimate Guide, Case Study, Deep Dive, Thought Leadership, Industry Trends

**Spotlight Pal (10 items, 2 categories):**
- Professional Productions (5): Company Overview, Executive Interviews, Culture Video, Event Coverage, Product Launch
- Brand Story (5): Brand Documentary, Origin Story, Values Video, Mission Statement, Impact Story

**System Pal (10 items, 2 categories):**
- Training Videos (5): Employee Onboarding, Safety Training, Software Tutorial, Standard Procedures, Compliance Training
- Internal Communications (5): Company Updates, Announcements, Town Hall Recording, Department Overview, Quarterly Review

### Page Sections (top to bottom)
1. **Back to Dashboard** button
2. **Pal Header**: Emoji + name + description + industry badge
3. **SystemCompletionCard**: Same cross-Pal progress widget as the main dashboard
4. **Video Production Progress**: Per-Pal progress bar showing "X of Y videos filmed"
5. **Quick Actions**: 3 buttons linking to AI tools (Write 5 Hooks → Series Builder, Plan 10 Shorts → Content System Builder, Engagement Starters → Engagement Responder)
6. **Video Production Checklist**: Checkbox list grouped by category with counters (e.g., "0/6"). Clicking a checkbox upserts to `user_video_checklist` table
7. **Pro Tips Card**: 3 static tips per Pal (e.g., "Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds" for Reel)
8. **Achievement Popup**: When checking off items triggers a milestone (25%/50%/75%/100%), confetti fires and an achievement card appears showing the unlocked badge name, description, and points

### Data Flow
- Checklist state is persisted in `user_video_checklist` (columns: `user_id`, `pal`, `video_id`, `completed`, `completed_at`)
- On toggle, a DB trigger `check_and_unlock_achievements` runs server-side to insert into `user_achievements` if milestones are hit
- The client polls for new achievements 500ms after toggle and shows the confetti celebration if a new one is detected
