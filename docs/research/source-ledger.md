# Palmer House research source ledger

Last editorial review: 2026-10-07. This is an internal fact-checking record for the Research category of Field Guides. It is not a keyword-volume report.

## Rules for each page
- Start with a question that searchers and writers actually ask. Verify Google Ads search estimates and competing-page referring domains before committing to a larger batch.
- Link to the original study beside each relevant section. Do not treat an aggregator repeating a figure as an independent source.
- Record the surveyed population, fieldwork date, geography, metric and denominator. Distinguish marketer responses, business responses and consumer responses.
- Never call compiled data an original Palmer House study. Do not describe associations as causal effects.
- Review source pages each quarter and when the publisher releases a new report. Change the visible reviewed date only after an actual review.
- Cite the original publisher even when Palmer House presents a table, summary or visualization.
