# Expo example photography

These are illustrative stock photographs, not photographs of the fictional businesses in Example mode, Palmer House clients, or campaign results generated by the product. They do not imply a photographer, business, or depicted person's endorsement.

All 12 photographs were downloaded from free Unsplash photo pages (not Unsplash+) on September 30, 2026. Each source page identifies the image as free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits commercial and noncommercial use. Photographer credits are retained here for attribution and provenance.

The JPGs are locally bundled so Example mode does not depend on a third-party image host once cached. Images were requested from the Unsplash CDN at 1,500 pixels wide, JPEG quality 82, with original aspect ratios preserved. No logos, text, or other graphics were added to the photographs.
